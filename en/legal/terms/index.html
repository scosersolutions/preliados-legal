3. Playing without an account and playing with an account

You do not need an account to play. When you open the app, an anonymous session tied to your device is created automatically, without asking you to register or provide an email or password. That session is stored in the system's secure storage (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android), so it normally survives reinstalling the app on the same device. It is what allows us to remember your purchases and your settings.

To take part in the community —publishing, rating or reporting decks— you do need an account. You can create one with email and password, with Google or with Apple. The reason is simple: published content must be attributable to someone who can answer for it, edit it or remove it, and an anonymous session tied to a phone does not offer that guarantee.

Creating the account links your anonymous session instead of replacing it, so you keep your purchases, your decks and your settings.

  • You are responsible for keeping your credentials confidential and for all activity that takes place under your account.
  • You must provide a valid email address that belongs to you. Creating accounts by impersonating another person is not permitted.
  • We may suspend or cancel an account that seriously or repeatedly breaches these terms or the community guidelines, following the procedure described there.

You can delete your account whenever you want from Settings → YOUR ACCOUNT → Delete my account, without asking us and without giving any explanation. Please note that doing so also deletes all the decks you have published. The details are in Delete your account and your data, and your rights over that data are in the privacy policy.
