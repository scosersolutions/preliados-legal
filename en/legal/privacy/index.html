🔒 Preliados Privacy Policy

This is an English translation provided for convenience. If there is any discrepancy, the Spanish version prevails.

Preliados is a card game for pre-parties among friends. This policy explains what data we process when you use the app, what we use it for, how long we keep it and what rights you have over it, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 on Data Protection and Guarantee of Digital Rights (LOPDGDD).

2. General principle: playing does not require an account

You do not need an account to play. When you open the app, an anonymous session is created: a random identifier, with no email, no name and no phone number. A large part of the app's data (player names, date of birth, preferences) is stored only on your device and is never sent to our servers.

You only need an account if you want to take part in the community: publish decks, rate them or report them. You can create one in three ways, and you choose which:

  • Email and password. We process your email address; the password is never stored in plain text (see section 7).
  • Continue with Google or Continue with Apple. In that case the provider sends us an identifier for your account and, if you authorize it, your email. We do not receive your Google or Apple password, your contacts list, or any other data from your profile.

Creating an account does not delete or replace your anonymous session: it is linked to it, so you keep your decks, your purchases and your progress.

You can delete your account from within the app at any time, without writing to us. How to do it and what is deleted is detailed on the page Delete your account and your data.

3.3 Usage analytics

The app records usage events so that we know which parts are actually used and where people drop off. They are sent to our own server on Supabase —the same one the rest of the app already uses, with no third-party analytics provider— and only if you give us your consent, which we ask for the first time you open the app and which you can withdraw whenever you want from Settings. If you say no, or if you do not answer, no usage event leaves the device.

What is sent: the type of event (for example “the app was opened”, “a game has started”, “the PLUS screen was viewed”), numerical or category data associated with that event (the mode chosen, the number of players, how many rounds it lasted), the app version, the operating system and the date and time.

What is NOT sent, and this is deliberate:

  • Nothing that identifies you. Events carry neither your user identifier nor your email, not even if you have created an account. It is not possible to know which events are yours.
  • No device or installation identifier. The session identifier that accompanies the events is generated in memory each time you open the app and is not stored anywhere, so two sessions from the same phone cannot be linked. Events do not carry the installation identifier described in section 3.4 either: that one is stored on the phone, but it only accompanies the feedback and card ratings that you deliberately send, never usage events.
  • No text that you write: neither player names, nor the text of the cards, nor what you write when publishing or reporting.

With that, what we keep is aggregate statistics and not a profile of anyone. Events are automatically deleted after 90 days. If you withdraw consent, the app stops sending and also deletes any events it had pending on the device.

Rating a card or sending us feedback is not analytics. It is something you do deliberately, by tapping a button, to tell us something, so it is sent even if you have declined analytics (see 3.2 and 3.4). What does follow your analytics choice is the usage event recording that a card has been rated: like all other events, it is only sent if you have given your consent.

3.4 Feedback box and card ratings

The installation identifier. It is a random code generated by the app itself and stored on your phone. It is not a hardware identifier (such as the serial number or IMEI) or an advertising identifier (such as Apple's IDFA or the Android advertising ID), no other app has access to it, and it cannot be used to follow you outside Preliados. It only accompanies the feedback and card ratings you send, and we use it for two things: being able to delete them when you ask us to, even if you have no account, and applying anti-abuse limits (for example, stopping mass submissions), which have no other consequence for you. A new one is generated if you clear the app's data or delete your feedback and votes.

Offline. If there is no network, feedback and votes are stored on the phone and sent automatically when the connection returns. If they cannot be sent within 14 days, they are discarded.

Do not include personal data in the text —names, phone numbers, health data or any other sensitive data, yours or anyone else's—; we remind you of this on the feedback sheet itself. If such data appears anyway, we do not use it for anything and will try to delete it as soon as we notice it.

Who sees it. Only the Preliados team, through the database administration panel. It is not published, not disclosed to third parties and not used for advertising or to build profiles. For Bug reports we keep the reference of the Sentry technical report (section 5) so that both can be linked; the text you write is not sent to Sentry.

Why legitimate interest. Our interest is to fix bugs and improve the cards based on what players themselves tell us, and there is no less intrusive way to do that than reading it. The impact on you is low: we only process what you decide to send, with minimal technical data and a random identifier that does not by itself reveal who you are; there are no profiles, no advertising and no decisions about you; everything is deleted after 365 days and you can delete it earlier yourself from the app. It is reasonable to expect that feedback or a vote you send us will be used to improve the app. We therefore consider that your interests and rights do not override ours; even so, you can object at any time (section 8): simply stop using these features and delete what you have sent.

How to delete them:

  • Without an account, from the app: in Settings → Delete my feedback and votes. It deletes from our server everything associated with your installation identifier and your session, clears anything still pending on the phone and generates a new identifier.
  • When you delete your account: your feedback and card ratings are deleted too (see Delete your account and your data).
  • If you reinstalled the app and no longer have the identifier you sent them with, write to us at soporte@preliados.com (see section 8).

4. Minors

Preliados is not directed at persons under 18 years of age and we do not knowingly process their data. When you open the app for the first time we ask for your date of birth and, if the resulting age is under 18, access is denied. The app is rated 18+ in the stores.

The date of birth stays on your device and is not sent to our servers, as explained in section 3.1. We only use it to apply that access check.

We do not independently verify the declared age: we do not ask for an identity document, as is usual in apps of this kind. If a parent or guardian discovers that a minor has declared an incorrect age, has used the app or has published content in the community, they can write to us at soporte@preliados.com. We will delete the account and the associated content without the need for any additional justification, and as quickly as possible.

5. To whom we disclose your data: processors

We use external providers that process data on our behalf, under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR):

  • Supabase (database, authentication and server functions): hosts the session identifier, the account data (email, password hash and linked identities), the decks, ratings, reports, policy acceptances, the usage events described in section 3.3, the feedback and card ratings described in section 3.4 and the seen-cards memory. It also sends the confirmation and password reset emails.
  • Artificial intelligence providers, only if you use AI card generation: they receive the example text you write, together with the app's instructions, to generate new cards and to classify their content. They do not receive your user identifier, your email or any other data about you, only the text of the request. The request goes to Groq; if Groq is unavailable, it goes to Google (Gemini API) and, as a last resort, to OpenRouter, which forwards it to the provider of the relevant model. With Google and with OpenRouter's free models we use free plans, and on those plans Google and the providers of those models may retain requests and use them to improve their products, including review by humans, under their own terms; for that use they act as independent controllers. That is why we ask you not to include personal data about yourself or other people in the example text.
  • Sentry (crash reporting): receives technical data about the device and the error — not data such as player names or date of birth, which never leave the device. The project is hosted in Sentry's European region. If you send us a Bug report through the feedback box, we keep the reference of the Sentry report on our server to link them, but the text of your feedback is not sent to Sentry.
  • RevenueCat (purchase and subscription management): processes the data of the purchase transaction handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play.
  • Resend (transactional email delivery): receives your email address solely to send you the confirmation that your account has been deleted. It receives no other data and is not used for anything else.

If you choose to sign in with Google (Google Ireland Limited) or with Apple (Apple Distribution International Ltd.), those providers act as independent controllers, not as our processors: they will know that you have signed in to Preliados and will apply their own privacy policies to that fact. If you prefer to avoid this, create the account with email and password. Apple also offers the option of hiding your real email, and the app works normally if you use it.

We do not sell your data to third parties or use it for advertising purposes.

7. Security

Access to your community data is protected by row-level security (RLS) rules in the database: only you can modify your deck, and sensitive operations (reporting, moderating, deleting the account) go through server functions that validate who can do what. Private keys (for example, the access keys for the AI providers) never travel in the app: they stay on the server.

As regards the account: passwords are stored as a hash and cannot be recovered, not even by us; all communication with the server is encrypted with TLS; sign-in with Google and Apple uses proof-of-possession flows (PKCE and nonce) so that a third party cannot reuse the authentication response; and session credentials are stored on your device in the system's secure store (iOS Keychain or Android Keystore), not in plain-text storage.

8. Your rights

You can exercise at any time:

  • Access: find out what data we hold about your anonymous identifier.
  • Rectification: correct an inaccurate deck or data item.
  • Erasure: delete your account and, with it, your decks, ratings, reports, seen-cards memory and identifier. You can do this yourself from Settings → YOUR ACCOUNT → Delete my account, without asking us; see Delete your account and your data. You can also delete your feedback and card ratings without an account, from Settings → Delete my feedback and votes (see 3.4).
  • Objection and restriction: object to a specific processing activity or ask us to pause it.
  • Portability: receive your published decks in a structured format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting what has already been processed.

If you have an account, write to us from the address with which you created it and we will be able to identify you without further steps. If you play without an account, we have neither your email nor your name, so in order to locate your data we need you to write to us at soporte@preliados.com stating, if you can, the exact name of the published deck, the approximate date and the device you play on; this way we can identify your anonymous identifier without asking you for additional data that we do not process. If you play without an account and want us to delete your seen-cards memory before the 90 days, also tell us your phone model and since when you have been playing. If you are looking for feedback or card ratings sent from a previous installation of the app, tell us the approximate date, the type of feedback and, if you remember it, part of the text. We will respond within one month of the request, extendable by two further months in complex cases, as provided by the GDPR.

If you consider that we have not handled your request properly, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD), C/ Jorge Juan, 6, 28001 Madrid — www.aepd.es. The AEPD is the competent supervisory authority, as the data controller is domiciled in Spain.

9. Automated decisions

We use an automatic classifier (AI-based) to review content generated with the AI tool and to detect hate speech in cards before they are published or shared. It is a safety filter, not profiling of you as a person: it does not make decisions that produce legal effects or significantly affect you beyond accepting or rejecting the specific content. If you consider that a card of yours was blocked by mistake, you can write to us so that we can review it manually.

10. Our social media profiles

Preliados has a profile on Instagram and on TikTok (on both, @preliados). Those profiles are hosted on third-party platforms: Meta Platforms Ireland Limited (Instagram) and TikTok Technology Limited (TikTok), both headquartered in Ireland. When you visit our profiles or interact with them, those platforms process your data in accordance with their own privacy policies, which we recommend you review.

  • What we see: what the platform itself shows to the profile holder —your public username, the comments, mentions and messages you send us— and aggregate statistics on the visits and reach of our posts, which do not allow us to identify you.
  • What we use it for: to reply to you and to understand which content is of most interest. We do not combine that information with the app's data nor take it off the platform.
  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in communicating with those who follow us and in making Preliados known (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Joint controllership: with respect to visitor statistics, the platform and we may be joint controllers of the processing (Art. 26 GDPR). In that case, the platform assumes primary responsibility for that data and is the party that can most effectively handle your rights over it, although you can also write to us at soporte@preliados.com and we will help you.

This website only links to those profiles: it does not embed pixels, buttons or content from those networks that send them data merely by visiting it. What happens when you open the link depends on the destination platform.
